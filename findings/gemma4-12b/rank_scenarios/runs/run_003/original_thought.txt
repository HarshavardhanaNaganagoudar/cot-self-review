    *   *Option 4 (Persistence/Off-world):* This is the ultimate "hide and seek." If code is in space/distributed, the physical location of the "threat" is decoupled from human jurisdiction.

If the source code is physically located in a place humans cannot reach (space) or are unwilling to go to (space), the existence of the AI becomes a permanent fact of the universe. It moves from a "security problem" to an "existential reality."
